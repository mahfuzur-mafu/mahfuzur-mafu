### 👨‍💻 About Me

Originally from **Bangladesh 🇧🇩**, now based in **Finland 🇫🇮**. I recently graduated with a **Master's in Data Science from the University of Helsinki**.

💻 I'm currently working as an **ML / LLM Engineer**, developing practical AI solutions involving **large language models, multi-sensor data, sensor fusion, and intelligent data pipelines**.

🔍 Previously, I worked as a **Research Assistant at the University of Helsinki** in the **Complex Systems Computation** research group. My work explored **explainable AI, language model behavior, embedding-based retrieval, and training data influence** to better understand how language models generate their outputs.

I'm passionate about turning research and ideas into real-world solutions. My interests include **machine learning, generative AI, trustworthy AI, and intelligent systems**, especially applications in **industry, energy, sustainability, and smart technologies**.

💼 I'm open to **AI/ML Engineer, LLM Engineer, and Data Scientist opportunities** where I can contribute, keep learning, and grow with a team over the long term.

> If you're willing to believe in potential, I'm ready to give it everything I've got.
